## 💡 ResQLife Kya Hai aur Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

ResQLife ek unified digital healthcare operating system hai jo chhote clinics, hospital reception desk aur aam public ke beech emergency logistics ka bridge banata hai[cite: 1].

Aamtaur par chhote clinics me appointments paper register ya WhatsApp par manage hote hain, emergency ke waqt ambulance ka live status nahi milta, blood group dhoondhne me ghanto nikal jaate hain, aur clinic se dawa lene ke baad patient sahi time par dose lena bhool jaate hain. 

**ResQLife is manual chaos ko 4 core automated workflows me badal deta hai:**

1. **Patient Desk:** Patient bina lambi line me lage turant digital token (`A-101`) aur printable pass generate karta hai[cite: 1, 2].
2. **Clinic Queue Console:** Receptionist ek click se OPD status track aur update karta hai (`Waiting` ➔ `In-Clinic` ➔ `Completed`)[cite: 1, 2].
3. **Emergency Dispatch:** Emergency aane par system severity ke hisab se ambulance unit allocate karke Google Maps route provide karta hai[cite: 1, 2].
4. **Life-Sync Alarm Vault:** Doctor/Reception dwara enter kiye gaye prescription timings par patient ke device me loud browser audio alert bachta hai, jo tabhi band hota hai jab patient dose confirmation kare[cite: 1, 2].
